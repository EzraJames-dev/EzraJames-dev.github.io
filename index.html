Malone Painting Assistant

Internal operations tool for Malone Painting, a residential painting company in St. Louis, Missouri.

What it does

Malone Painting Assistant helps our team keep up with customers. When a Malone Painting team member signs in with Google, the app can:

  • Gmail: read and draft estimate follow-ups and customer replies, and send messages on the team member's behalf.
  • Google Calendar: check availability and schedule estimate appointments and follow-up calls.

The app is used only by Malone Painting staff to manage their own accounts. It isn't offered to the public.

Your data

We only access the Google data needed for these features. We never sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details.
