Privacy Policy

Malone Painting Assistant · Effective September 23, 2026

This policy explains how Malone Painting Assistant (“the App”), operated by Malone Painting in St. Louis, Missouri, accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google user data. The App is an internal tool used by Malone Painting staff.

1. Data we access

With your permission through Google Sign-In, the App may access:

  • Basic profile: your name and email address, to identify your account.
  • Gmail: message content, metadata, and labels needed to read, draft, and send customer communications for you.
  • Google Calendar: events and free/busy information needed to view availability and create or update appointments.

2. How we use it

We use Google user data only to provide the App's user-facing features: managing customer email follow-ups and scheduling appointments. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to build profiles for unrelated purposes.

3. Sharing

We do not sell Google user data or share it with third parties. We transfer data only when the following apply:

  • It is needed to provide the App's features, for example sending data to service providers that process it for us under confidentiality obligations.
  • It is required by law.
  • You explicitly ask us to.

No person reads your data unless you consent, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Malone Painting Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Storage and security

OAuth tokens and any data we keep are stored on secured systems with restricted access and encrypted in transit. We keep data only as long as the App needs it to provide its features.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have your stored data deleted, email us at the address below. We will delete it within 30 days.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page and change the effective date.
